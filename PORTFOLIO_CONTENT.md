## 7. Project Challenges

1. The dataset does not contain customer-level or date-level columns, so the analysis focuses on transaction, product, geographic, segment, shipping, profitability, and discount dimensions.
2. Numeric fields and derived metrics needed safe handling for missing values, invalid values, and zero sales.
3. Multiple sidebar filters had to update every KPI, chart, table, insight, and download consistently.
4. Empty or highly restrictive filter results needed clear messages instead of broken charts or calculations.

## 8. Interview Questions and Answers

### 1. Why did you choose this project?

I chose retail analysis because it combines practical business questions with important Data Analyst skills such as cleaning, aggregation, visualization, and dashboard development.

### 2. What is included in the dataset?

The dataset includes sales, profit, quantity, discount, category, sub-category, region, state, city, segment, shipping mode, country, and postal code.

### 3. What cleaning did you perform?

I validated the columns, converted numeric fields safely, checked missing values and duplicates, and handled invalid values during analysis.

### 4. How did you use Pandas?

I used Pandas for loading data, filtering records, grouping dimensions, aggregating metrics, sorting results, calculating KPIs, and preparing chart data.

### 5. Which KPIs did you calculate?

The dashboard calculates total sales, total profit, profit margin, quantity sold, average discount, average profit per record, and record count.

### 6. How did you calculate profit margin?

Profit margin is calculated as total profit divided by total sales, multiplied by 100. The calculation safely handles zero sales.

### 7. How did you analyze discounts?

I compared discount values and discount ranges with sales, profit, quantity, profit margin, and record counts.

### 8. Why did you use Plotly?

Plotly provides interactive, responsive charts with hover information and clear comparisons across business dimensions.

### 9. Why did you use Streamlit?

Streamlit allowed me to present the analysis as an interactive application with filters, KPI cards, tables, expanders, and downloads.

### 10. How are business recommendations generated?

Recommendations are based on calculated results from the currently filtered data, such as weak profitability, loss-making areas, or high-discount patterns.
